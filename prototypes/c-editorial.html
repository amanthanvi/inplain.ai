Google widened what counts as your “media”, and said the quiet part out loud

Between 2026-04-02 and 2026-08-03, one collected category grew from voice and audio to images, files, audio and video — and a new sentence states that Google collects data not tied to your account at all.

18 passages changed +7,660 added −6,444 removed 93.6% unchanged

What changed

  • Google broadened what it collects from your interactions: the category that was “Voice and audio information” is now “Media (like images, files, audio and video) from your interactions”. [H3]
  • A new sentence states plainly that Google collects and uses data not associated with your account, where the previous wording only described what happens when you are signed out. [H2]
  • Activity you can find and manage is now described as saved “in your history” rather than “by your activity controls”. [H4]
